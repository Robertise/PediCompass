# General Newborn Care (0-28 Days)

## Symptoms
- Normal newborn behaviors include frequent waking for feeds, crying when hungry or uncomfortable, and sleeping for significant portions of the day.
- Mild color changes (e.g., slight yellowing/jaundice) that resolve or do not spread to palms/soles.

## Warning Signs (Red Flags)
- **Breathing Difficulties:** Fast breathing, difficult breathing, or severe chest indrawing.
- **Temperature Abnormalities:** Baby feels abnormally hot (fever) or abnormally cold (hypothermia).
- **Feeding Issues:** Refusal to feed or not feeding well.
- **Neurological Signs:** Convulsions, fits, twitching, or stiffening.
- **Movement/Activity:** Reduced activity, extreme lethargy, unconsciousness, or no movement at all.
- **Signs of Infection:** Pus or spreading redness around the umbilical cord, eyes, or skin pustules.
- **Severe Jaundice:** Yellowing that extends to the palms of the hands or soles of the feet.

## Care Actions
- **Thermal Protection:** Keep the baby warm, ideally through skin-to-skin contact. Avoid excessive exposure to cold.
- **Hygiene:** Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water before handling the baby. Keep the umbilical cord clean and dry; do not apply unprescribed ointments.
- **Feeding:** Initiate early, exclusive breastfeeding. Feed on demand.
- **Immediate Action:** If *any* "Red Flag" danger signs are observed, seek immediate medical care at a health facility. Do not delay.
- **Routine Care:** Ensure the baby receives recommended postnatal check-ups (e.g., days 1, 3, 7-14, and at 6 weeks) and timely vaccinations.
